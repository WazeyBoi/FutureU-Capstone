1. Bachelor of Arts in International Studies

The Bachelor of Arts in International Studies is a four-year interdisciplinary program that combines rigorous coursework in history, political science, foreign relations, economics, international languages, and socio-cultural affairs. Students develop deep conceptual knowledge through comparative analysis of global political systems, examination of diplomatic case studies, and cross-cultural communication training. The curriculum emphasizes critical thinking methodologies, geopolitical trend analysis, and problem-solving approaches to understand complex international dynamics and global relationships while building intercultural competence essential for international engagement.

Graduates may pursue careers as foreign service officers, diplomatic liaisons, international relations specialists, policy advisors, and program coordinators in NGOs and multinational corporations. They develop specialized skills in diplomatic communication, cross-cultural negotiation, leadership in international contexts, and strategic problem-solving for global challenges. Career opportunities span government agencies, international organizations, non-governmental institutions, foreign service, and multinational corporations. Career advancement in government service typically requires passing Civil Service Examinations, while Foreign Service positions require specialized diplomatic examinations administered by the Department of Foreign Affairs. The program provides strong foundation for graduate studies in international relations, diplomatic studies, and related fields, with growing demand for professionals who can navigate intercultural competence and global relationship management in an increasingly interconnected world.

The curriculum emphasizes theoretical learning combined with applied research methodologies, psychological assessment techniques, and evidence-based approaches to behavioral analysis, preparing students to adhere to professional codes of ethics in their practice.

Graduates may pursue diverse careers in academic settings as educators, counselors, test administrators, and researchers, or in government service as public relations officers, rehabilitation and probation specialists, health and social welfare workers, and crime detection personnel. With increasing focus on biodiversity conservation, climate change research, and biotechnological innovations, biology graduates are increasingly valued in emerging fields such as bioinformatics, conservation biology, systems biology, and biomedical research, offering diverse career pathways in both public and private sectors.
